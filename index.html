折叠书 · 人類歷史

一本全手工装帧的折叠书：书页以风琴折连续展开——从丝绸之路上缓缓行进的驼队，到古代文明的转轮、蒸汽与航天飞机的时代，再到太空。人类文明的重要时刻被依次折进一条可以一路走完的时间线，折痕即是章节：每展开一折，历史便翻开一面。

A hand-bound concertina folding book. From the camel caravans of the Silk Road to the wheel of empires, the age of steam and the spaceflight era — milestones of civilization folded into one continuous timeline. Every crease is a chapter: each fold turns a page of history.

CHENKAI (KEVIN) SHOU PORTFOLIO · 2023

A handmade artist's book that unfolds into a spatial narrative — weaving memory, displacement & the quiet poetry of everyday detours.

A single-edition pop-up book that turns reading into a journey you walk through: folds, joints and pop-ups that release the prose of a young migrant slowly making a home in a new city.

Concept

A single-edition pop-up book, built entirely by hand. The essay is told through paper mechanics rather than flat pages — folds, tabs and joints that make the act of reading a physical, spatial journey.

Narrative

Diary-like prose panels drift across the folds. Fragments of memory, homesickness and everyday small detours slowly accumulate, quietly reshaping a place that is both home and elsewhere.

Structure

An accordion spine carries die-cut silhouettes and liftable pop-ups. As the book unfolds, panels rise and collapse, opening the text into a walkable, three-dimensional reading.

Material

Black and navy board with screen-printed panels and hand-cut paper. Every mechanism — hinge, tab, fold — is finished by hand, keeping the precision of an edition of one.

CHENKAI (KEVIN) SHOU HANFU · PROJECT 04 — MOVING IMAGE

Projection · Installation · Garment

Han·Fu

Traditional Chinese dress — stepped into a small room, dressed in light, sound & moving shadow.

Hanfu is a cross-medium piece that fuses projection art, installation and garment design. Its subject is 汉服 — the traditional dress of ancient China — retold without the museum hush. The mood should feel less like an archive and more like browsing an old garment website: playful enough to keep you curious, gentle enough to teach the cut and character of Chinese traditional clothing along the way.

01
From Zero to Seam

Learned sewing from absolute zero and drafted authentic Hanfu patterns (版型), finishing a garment built to hold its own under projected light.

02
Play with the Space

The room was small and unforgiving. Every object's size and position decided whether the piece breathed or collapsed — placement became the composition.

03
Sight & Sound

A visual and aural experience: the image moves, the room speaks, and the audience picks up the features of Chinese traditional dress without ever being lectured.
